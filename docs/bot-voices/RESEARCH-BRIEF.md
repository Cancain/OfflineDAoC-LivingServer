# Bot voices: research brief (Classic + Shrouded Isles, 2001–2003)

This brief is the material behind the gamebots' new lines. It records what players of the period
talked about, how they talked, where they played, and what they argued over. The lines themselves
are new writing: no forum post is copied, and no real player, guild or person is named.

**Verified** means checked in a cited source, the world database or the server code.
**Period knowledge** means it comes from general knowledge of the game and has **not** been checked
against a period source. Those items are the first to confirm when old forum threads are added.

## 1. Rules for the lines

- **Era:** the game as it stood from launch (9 October 2001) to version 1.65 (8 October 2003), the
  last patch before Trials of Atlantis (28 October 2003). *Verified:* Wikipedia, Camelot Herald wiki.
- **Out of era** (the validator rejects these):
  - Trials of Atlantis: Atlantis, artifacts, master levels;
  - New Frontiers (June 2004): its towers, the new frontier and its keeps;
  - Catacombs (December 2004): its classes, races and champion levels.
- **No copying:** forum posts belong to their authors. They are used for topics and arguments; every
  line is written new.
- **No real people:** no real player handles, guild names, developers or server names.
- **Clean language:** no profanity. Period chat was saltier, but the plan's rule stands.

## 3. How people played together (what the lines should feel like)

**Verified (retrospective and community sources):**
- **Camps were social.** People hunted one spawn for hours, and talked while they waited for
  respawns.
- **Scarce good groups.** A good group was hard to find, so people kept it and remembered who was in
  it.
- **Spawn competition.** Nothing was instanced: people asked whether a camp was taken, and argued
  about kill-stealing.
- **Word of mouth.** Reputation and legends spread by talk: who was a great healer, who trained a
  group, where a named monster was.
- **Slow levels made milestones events.** Hitting a level ("ding") brought congratulations.
- **Buffbots were a long-running argument** (second accounts that buffed and healed a main).
- **Relics followed population:** players said the biggest realm on a server held them.

**Period knowledge (to confirm):**
- **Grouping talk** carried class, level and role: "LFG 34 healer", "LF tank and MA for Spindelhalla".
- **Sitting and waiting** for health or power ("med", "sitting for pow") was a natural pause in chat.
- **Trading** happened in capitals and by shouting wares. Prices were in gold and platinum.
- **Advice:** newer players asked about specs and camps, and veterans answered, sometimes curtly.

## 4. How people wrote

**Verified period slang:** LFG, LFM, OOC, BRB, AFK, twisting (bards weaving songs), KoS, zerg,
ninja looting.

**Period knowledge (in common use, to confirm wording):**

| Area | Terms |
|---|---|
| Grouping | lfg, lfm, pst, inv pls, MA, assist, puller, "need heals", full group (fg), 8man |
| Combat | inc, add(s), train, pull, aggro, mez, root, snare, stun, kite, oom, oop ("out of power"), rez, buffs, pbaoe, dd, dot, lifetap, pet, LoS |
| Monsters | con colors (grey, green, blue, yellow, orange, red, purple), named, spawn, pop, camp, ks |
| Social | ding, grats / gz, ty, np, lol, heh, rofl, brb, afk, gtg, omw, gl, :) |
| RvR | rr / rps, realm abilities (RAs), zerg, gank, stealther, keep, lord, door, ram, relic, DF, BG, twink |
| Gear and craft | MP (masterpiece), 99%, SC (spellcraft), imbue, overcharge, legendary (skill 1000), alchemy, dye |

- **Style:** mostly lowercase, little punctuation, abbreviations, "u"/"ur" from some players.
- **Emoticons:** ":)" and ":P".
- **Ellipsis:** for waiting ("lfg..."), plus occasional all-caps for alarms ("INC").

**Reputations in 2002–2003 (period knowledge, to confirm):**
- **Healers and casters:**
  - "pac" Healers and Sorcerers for mez;
  - Shamans for disease and debuffs ("cave" Shamans for damage);
  - Clerics as Albion's main healers;
  - Druids and Bards as Hibernia's heal and mez support.
- **Bards and Minstrels:** speed and twisting songs.
- **Archers:** Scouts' range was a frequent complaint.
- **Stealthers** (Infiltrator, Shadowblade, Nightshade): solo hunters of lone players in the frontier.
- **Pets:**
  - Bonedancers, Theurgists, Cabalists and Necromancers;
  - Animists' mushrooms ("shrooms").
- **Tanks and melee:**
  - Berserkers and Savages for melee damage;
  - Armsmen and Heroes as tanks;
  - Valewalkers and Reavers as newer Shrouded Isles classes people were still figuring out.
- **Caster damage:** Wizards, Runemasters and Eldritch.

that isn't in your world.

## 7. Recurring arguments (period knowledge, to confirm)

Each becomes a short scene with two sides. The lines are new and keep both sides' real points.

1. **Buffbots:** smart use of a second account, or an unfair one-person group?
2. **Stealthers:** ruining RvR for lone players, or the frontier's natural predators?
3. **Archers' range:** overpowered, or easy to close on?
4. **The zerg:** big realm fights versus small groups; is zerging "real" RvR?
5. **Relics:** the biggest realm always wins them; does relic power matter?
6. **Darkness Falls access:** "our realm has DF" is worth fighting for, or a PvE crutch.
7. **Twinks in Thidranki:** fun, or unfair to new players?
8. **Spellcraft:** a full SC'd suit is a must at 50, or a money sink.
9. **Crafting:** grinding to legendary is a money pit; masterpieces are worth it, or not.
10. **Pac healer or mending healer?** Which specialization helps a group more.
11. **Solo classes:** which class solos best; pets versus everything else.
12. **Leveling pace:** rushing 50 or enjoying the camps.
13. **Kill-stealing and camp etiquette:** who was here first; can we share?
14. **Training:** someone ran a train of monsters onto a group.
15. **Shrouded Isles zones:** worth leveling there, or are the classic zones better?
16. **Nerfs:** a patch nerfed my class; everyone else's class is overpowered.
17. **Realm pride:** Albion's casters, Midgard's numbers, Hibernia's mez and bards, as friendly
    rivalry.
18. **Guilds:** big RvR guilds versus small family guilds.
19. **Lag in big fights:** frame rates in Emain during a siege.
20. **New-player advice:** "don't rush, you'll miss the camps" versus "level fast, the game starts at
    50".

**Mechanics used in lines but not yet confirmed** (period knowledge; first things to check):
- **Death:**
  - praying at your grave to win back some experience;
  - constitution loss after deaths, cured by a healer in town;
  - experience can go down (lines say the bar "goes backwards" and never claim a lost level).

  - `/bind` at a bind stone;
  - boats to the Shrouded Isles.
- **Respecs exist** (lines never say how or when).
- **Class details:**
  - disease reduces healing;
  - Savages fight with claw-like hand-to-hand weapons.
- **Rules of the world:**
  - players leave Thidranki at 25;
  - enemy-realm speech can't be understood.
